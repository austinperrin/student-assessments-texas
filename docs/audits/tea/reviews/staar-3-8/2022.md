### TEA-STAAR38-2022-001: Twelve item-level ranges disagree with the source

- Status: confirmed
- Severity: high
- Reviewer: Codex
- Verifier: Austin Perrin
- Disposition owner: Austin Perrin
- Target date: before family audit closure
- Source document: 2022 STAAR Grades 3-8 Data File Format
- Official URL: `https://tea.texas.gov/student-assessment/student-assessment-results/2022-staar-3-8-data-file-format.pdf`
- Source page/table: pages 10, 12, 13-15; subject item-level information
- Source positions/columns: 451-598, 801-954, 1601-1748, and 2001-2150
- Current mapping positions: mapped fields 134-136, 162-164, 186-188, and 202-204

#### Source fact

Reading uses three 44-byte item ranges, mathematics and science use three
42-byte ranges, and social studies uses three 44-byte ranges. The blank
separators shown in the source begin immediately after those ends.

#### Current implementation

The reading ranges each extend one byte into a source blank. The mathematics
ranges each omit their final two bytes; social studies and science each omit
their final four bytes. The science reporting-category range is also named as
strand numbers instead of reporting-category numbers.

#### Impact

Item values can be truncated or can consume blank bytes, and the science item
concept is mislabeled.

#### Proposed disposition

Correct all twelve ends and rename the science reporting-category field after
independent verification.

#### Verification

Austin Perrin independently reviewed and confirmed the cited source and mapping
differences on 2026-09-25.

### TEA-STAAR38-2022-003: Invented score fields occupy source blank and valid ranges

- Status: confirmed
- Severity: high
- Reviewer: Codex
- Verifier: Austin Perrin
- Disposition owner: Austin Perrin
- Target date: before family audit closure
- Source document: 2022 STAAR Grades 3-8 Data File Format
- Source page/table: pages 11 and 13-16; subject information and blank ranges
- Source positions/columns: source blanks 615-750, 978-1500, 1760-1900, and 2166-2500
- Current mapping positions: mapped fields 139, 168-171, 190-191, and 206-207

#### Source fact

The cited ranges are blank in the 2022 layout, except that valid previous-year
Reading history begins at position 2501.

#### Current implementation

The mapping places an extended-constructed-response field and eight
points-possible/points-achieved fields in these ranges. The Science points
achieved range, 2488-2525, overlaps twelve valid previous-year fields beginning
at 2501.

#### Impact

Blank bytes are presented as scores, and one invented range aliases valid
history data.

#### Proposed disposition

Remove the unsupported fields after independent verification and downstream
consumer review.

#### Verification

unsupported mapped fields, and overlap at positions 2501-2525 on 2026-09-25.
