### TEA-STAAR38-2020-002: Twelve item-level ranges disagree with the source

- Severity: high
- Reviewer: Codex
- Verifier: Austin Perrin
- Disposition owner: Austin Perrin
- Target date: before family audit closure
- Source page/table: pages 15, 18, and 23-25; subject item-level information
- Source positions/columns: 451-598, 801-954, 1601-1748, and 2001-2150
- Current mapping positions: mapped fields 134-136, 162-164, 186-188, and 202-204

#### Source fact

Reading and Social Studies use 44-byte item ranges. Mathematics and Science use
42-byte item ranges, with blank separators immediately after each range.

two bytes; Social Studies and Science ranges each omit four bytes. The Science
reporting-category range is also mislabeled as strand numbers. Values can be
truncated or consume blank bytes.

independent verification.

and the incorrect Science concept name on 2026-09-26.
