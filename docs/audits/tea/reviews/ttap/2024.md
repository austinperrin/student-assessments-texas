## Findings And Resolutions

### 1. Seven segment groups omitted form qualifiers

- State: `verified`
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: pages 3-7, positions 652-3171.
- Source fact: all four fields for each segment include a meaningful form qualifier: segment 1 is `Router/Landing Page`; segments 2 and 5 are `High`; segments 3 and 6 are `Medium`; and segments 4 and 7 are `Low`.
- Resolution: restored the source-supported qualifier to all 28 segment headers.

### 2. Predicted performance title was truncated

- State: `verified`
- Severity: low
- Evidence: page 1, positions 506-546.
- Source fact: the complete title is `Predicted STAAR Performance Level (Beta)`.
- Resolution: renamed the header to `predicted_staar_performance_level_beta`.

### 3. Two recurring concepts did not use required normalized names

- State: `verified`
- Severity: low
- Evidence: pages 8-9, positions 3201 and 3205, together with the repository normalization rules.
- Source fact: the LEP row describes the recurring emergent-bilingual status concept, and the gifted-talented row describes the recurring gifted-and-talented indicator concept.
- Resolution: renamed the headers to `emergent_bilingual_indicator_code` and `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code` without changing their positions or meanings.

### 4. Two accessibility headers omitted wrapped source text

- State: `verified`
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: page 9, positions 3220-3221.
- Source fact: the complete field titles are `Content-Language Accommodations` and `Braille/Refreshable Braille Device Screen Reader Support`.
- Resolution: restored the complete concepts as `content_language_accommodations` and `braille_refreshable_braille_device_screen_reader_support`.

In total, 33 source-supported header corrections were applied. No positional, width, ordering, ordinal, metadata, administration-code, or test-code changes were required.

## Filename Provenance And Limitations

No operational 2023-2024 TTAP result file or delivered filename sample was available, so filename matching and record-value validation could not be independently tested. This is a documented evidence limitation, not an accepted block on the source-layout audit.
