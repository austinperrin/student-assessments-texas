## Findings And Resolutions

### TFAR-2024-001: stale official PDF URL

- Severity: medium
- Source: TEA Data File Formats index, 2024 TFAR entry; official PDF pages 3-5
- Evidence: the prior URL returned HTTP 404. The current index links
  `2023-2024-tfar-data-file-1.pdf`, which is 152,046 bytes and byte-for-byte
  identical to the archive.
- Resolution: replaced `metadata.pdf_url` with the live indexed URL.
- Final status: corrected and verified by Reviewer 1

### TFAR-2024-002: gifted-and-talented conjunction omitted

- Severity: low
- Source: page 5, position 115 in the mapping
- Evidence: the field title is `Gifted-Talented-Indicator-Code`, and its note
  repeatedly identifies a `Gifted and Talented Program`. The shared
  normalization requires `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code` when that meaning
  is present.
- Resolution: changed `gifted_talented_indicator_code` to
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`.
- Final status: corrected and verified by Reviewer 1

### TFAR-2024-003: enrolled-grade header omitted source meaning

- Severity: low
- Source: page 5, positions 130-131 in the mapping
- Evidence: the source field is `Student-Enrolled-Grade-Code`; `enrolled_grade`
  omitted both `student` and `code`.
- Resolution: changed the header to `student_enrolled_grade_code`.
- Final status: corrected and verified by Reviewer 1

### TFAR-2024-004: percent-correct header omitted test qualifier

- Severity: low
- Source: page 5, positions 165-168 in the mapping
- Evidence: the source field is `Test Percent Correct`; `percent_correct`
  omitted the source's `Test` qualifier.
- Resolution: changed the header to `test_percent_correct`.
- Final status: corrected and verified by Reviewer 1

- TEA's published source leaves every Start, End, and Field Length cell empty.
  Two independent local rendering/extraction paths and TEA's indexed text view
  showed the same absence, and the current official PDF is byte-identical to the
  archive. Reviewer 1 could therefore prove the mapping ranges' internal
  consistency but could not independently source-verify their numeric values.
  Reviewer 2 independently reproduced this limitation and blocked the work
  unit. The program owner subsequently accepted the limitation under the
  exception policy; Reviewer 2 must confirm that exception documentation before
  the work unit can advance.
- The source does not document a delivered result-file filename. Its own PDF
  basename is documentation provenance, not evidence for a data-file regex.
- No operational sample was available. Width conformance, filename behavior,
  affected-record counts, and historical reprocessing impact are unknown.
