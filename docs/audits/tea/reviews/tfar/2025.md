## Findings And Resolutions

### 2025-TFAR-001 — Source qualifiers and supported normalization were omitted

- Severity: medium
- State: verified
- Affected layer: normalized headers
- Source evidence: PDF page 3, positions 48–78 labels the three fields
  `Student Last-Name`, `Student First-Name`, and `Student Middle-Name`. PDF
  page 4, position 122 labels `Gifted-Talented-Indicator-Code`, and its note
  explicitly describes participation in a “Gifted and Talented Program.”
- Baseline mapping: used `last_name`, `first_name`, `middle_name`, and
  `gifted_talented_indicator_code`.
- Interpretation: omitting `student` discards an explicit source qualifier.
  Adding `and` to the gifted-and-talented header follows the shared
  normalization and is independently supported by the same-year note.
- Correction: changed the four headers to `student_last_name`,
  `student_first_name`, `student_middle_name`, and
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code` without changing positions or ordinals.
- Validation: the corrected headers are unique lowercase snake case, are at
  most 63 characters, and preserve the source meanings.

## Limitations And Open Questions

- The official TEA URL returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-08, so the archived PDF
  could not be compared byte-for-byte with a current online copy.
- No operational sample was available. Delivered filename provenance and
  record-level parsing could not be tested, and the review does not add a
  speculative filename pattern.
- The PDF table has internal numeric inconsistencies on page 3: District ID is
  printed as positions 12–17 with length 5, while those endpoints span 6
  bytes; the two 15-byte student-name fields are printed with endpoints 48–52
  and 53–77. The mapping retains 12–17 for District ID and the uniquely
  continuous 15-byte interpretation 48–62 and 63–77 for the two names. This
  interpretation preserves the explicit starts/ends around the segment, the
  printed field lengths, and continuous coverage, but an operational sample
  was unavailable for independent confirmation.
