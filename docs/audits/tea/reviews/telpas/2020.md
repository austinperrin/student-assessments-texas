## Scope and Method

Reviewer 1 used only the assigned 2020 mapping, the same-year archived and
official source, and governing repository instructions. No other year's
mapping or source was used as audit evidence.

Every source-table row was reconstructed in position order using layout-aware
extraction and direct page inspection where headings or wrapped descriptions
could be confused with rows. Each row's printed range, length, name, code
meaning, and source ordinal was compared with the mapping. Blank rows were
counted in `column_num` and omitted from `mapped_fields`.

## Findings and Corrections

### Omitted named source rows and ordinal sequence

- Finding: The mapping omitted `SEX-CODE` at position 83 (ordinal 11),
  `DATE-OF-BIRTH (MMDDYYYY)` at positions 84-91 (ordinal 12), and
  `AMERICAN-INDIAN-ALASKA-NATIVE-CODE` at position 94 (ordinal 15).
- Correction: Added `sex_code`, `date_of_birth`, and
  `american_indian_alaska_native_code` at their documented ranges.
- Resolution: Recomputed every mapped ordinal from the 153-row source table.
  Position 93 is ordinal 14, position 95 is ordinal 16, and every mapping from
  position 95 through the terminal position 1200 moves three ordinals forward.
- State: corrected; awaiting Reviewer 2 verification.

### Source-supported header repair

- Finding: Truncated, placeholder, generated, or semantically incorrect headers
  obscured source meanings.
- Correction: Repaired 48 existing headers using the same-year source. This
  includes `hispanic_latino_code`, `asian_code`, the shared
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`, Agency Use columns A-E, the Fall 2019
  TSDS PEIMS campus and crisis fields, domain-qualified accommodation names,
  and plural listening, speaking, and reading reporting-category score names.
- History correction: Positions 1001-1039 are explicitly under **Spring 2018
  Administration**, not 2017; positions 1040-1072 are explicitly under
  **Spring 2019 Administration**, not 2018. All affected grade, campus, years
  in U.S. schools, domain score-code/rating/scale-score, and composite-rating
  headers now carry the documented year.
- Resolution: Names are unique lowercase snake case, remain within 63
  characters, and preserve distinct score-code, proficiency-rating,
  scale-score, holistic/paper administration, composite-score,
  composite-rating, and historical-administration concepts.
- State: corrected; awaiting Reviewer 2 verification.

### Filename evidence

- Finding: The source states that delivery is a fixed-length text file and that
  the extension **will be `.txt`**. The inherited patterns made that extension
  optional.
- Correction: All four inherited regular expressions now require `.txt`.
- Provenance limitation: The layout does not document delivered basenames.
  The inherited basename forms and their existing references are retained as
  provenance-limited patterns; no basename token was inferred from another
  year's layout. Administration code `0320` is confirmed by the source, but
  the layout does not independently establish every token accepted by the
  inherited patterns.
- State: corrected; awaiting Reviewer 2 verification.

- No operational 2020 result sample was available. Delivered basenames,
  record-level population behavior, and real-world regex matching could not be
  verified.

  Downstream consumers may require coordinated aliases or reprocessing under
  their local retention policy.
