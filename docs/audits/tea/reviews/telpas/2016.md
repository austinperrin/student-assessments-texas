## Findings And Resolutions

### TELPAS-2016-001 — blank source row was mapped as response data

- Severity: high
- Source evidence: page 11, positions 483-552, source ordinal 116, is explicitly `BLANK`; the student item response row is positions 553-622, ordinal 117.
- Finding: `reading_correct_item_response` incorrectly mapped the blank range at 483-552.
- Resolution: Removed the mapping entry. The blank source row remains represented by the ordinal gap, and `reading_student_item_response` remains at 553-622.
- Status: corrected and Reviewer 1 verified

### TELPAS-2016-002 — source ordinals omitted blank rows

- Severity: high
- Source evidence: the PDF contains 157 positional rows, including 34 rows explicitly labeled `BLANK`. The first blank is positions 92-92 at source ordinal 13, and the final `PERIOD` row at position 800 is source ordinal 157.
- Finding: the inherited `column_num` sequence compressed out blank rows and ended at 124 rather than preserving source-row ordinals.
- Resolution: Reassigned every mapped `column_num` from its source-row ordinal. This changed 111 mapped ordinals; the sequence now retains all blank-row gaps and ends with `period`, ordinal 157.
- Status: corrected and Reviewer 1 verified

### TELPAS-2016-003 — generated or incomplete headers obscured source meaning

- Severity: medium
- Source evidence: pages 3-10 distinguish Gifted/Talented, Agency Use columns A-E, Fall 2015 PEIMS fields, reading accommodations, plan-year ELL fields, current composite rating, and four reading proficiency-level score groups.
- Finding: 35 headers used generated position suffixes, an incorrect Agency Use label, incomplete wrapped titles, or omitted semantic qualifiers.
- Resolution: Replaced those headers with source-supported names. Notable corrections include `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`, `agency_use_a` through `agency_use_e`, `fall_2015_peims_county_district_campus_number`, reading-qualified accommodation names, plan-year-qualified fields, proficiency-level-qualified reporting-category names, and distinct plan-year/current TELPAS composite-rating names.
- Status: corrected and Reviewer 1 verified

### TELPAS-2016-004 — metadata and archive were not aligned to the current official source

- Severity: medium
- Source evidence: TEA's current archive listing links the 2016 TELPAS layout at the current `data-reports` URL.
- Finding: `metadata.pdf_url` cited a third-party Texas ERC copy, and the local archive was semantically equivalent but not byte-identical to the current TEA PDF.
- Resolution: Updated `metadata.pdf_url` to the working TEA URL and refreshed the same-year archive from that URL. The tracked archive now has SHA-256 `2B30B5946401318C8EBBBE481DFEE894376B0B3D1BEDAC1ADD657A711B72514F`, matching the official download.
- Status: corrected and Reviewer 1 verified
