## Findings And Corrections

### Final source ordinals counted a section heading as a row

- Finding: The mapping skipped from source ordinal 206 at position 1135 to
  ordinal 208 at position 1136. The intervening `Reference` text on page 17 is
  a section heading without a start, end, or length, not a source row. This
  made the final five mapped ordinals one too high.
- Correction: Positions 1136-1171, 1172-1180, 1181, 1182, and 1200 now use
  ordinals 207, 208, 209, 210, and 212, respectively. The blank at positions
  1183-1199 remains omitted as source ordinal 211.
- Resolution: corrected and ready for independent verification.

### Gifted and talented shared normalization was missing

- Finding: The page 4 field at position 117 is `Gifted-Talented-Indicator-Code`
  and its note describes participation in the state-approved Gifted/Talented
  Program. The mapping used `gifted_talented_indicator_code`.
- Correction: Normalized the header to
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`, preserving the documented meaning.
- Resolution: corrected and ready for independent verification.

### Official source URL had moved

- Finding: The inherited `/student-assessment/student-assessment-results/`
  URL returned HTTP 404. TEA's current data-file-formats page links the 2025
  TELPAS layout under `/data-reports/student-assessment-results/`.
- Correction: Updated `metadata.pdf_url` to the verified current URL. The
  downloaded official PDF matches the archive byte for byte.
- Resolution: corrected and ready for independent verification.

### Listening-response positions are internally inconsistent in the source

- Finding: The overview on page 2 says Listening Student Response by Item is
  at positions 869-900, and the page 12 note says item 1 begins at 869. The
  positional table on page 12 prints the field at 851-880 with length 30,
  followed by a 20-character blank at 881-900. The table's endpoints, lengths,
  and continuous row sequence agree with one another; the overview and note do
  not.
- Resolution: The mapping retains the internally consistent positional-table
  range 851-880 and omits blank positions 881-900. This source inconsistency is
  recorded for Reviewer 2; no unsupported range change was made.
