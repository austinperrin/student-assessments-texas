## Findings and Corrections

### Source terminology and shared normalizations

The following source-supported header corrections were applied:

- position 107: `lep_indicator_code` to
  `emergent_bilingual_indicator_code`, applying the required shared
  normalization to the source's LEP indicator concept;
- position 117: `gifted_talented_indicator_code` to
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`;
- positions 133-137: replaced five erroneous/generated Agency Use headers
  with `agency_use_a` through `agency_use_e`, matching source columns A-E;
- positions 155-163: replaced the positional placeholder with
  `fall_2020_tsds_peims_county_district_campus_number`;
- positions 174-175: removed an unsupported `_1` suffix from the single Fall
  2020 TSDS PEIMS crisis-code field;
- positions 221, 222, 241, and 243: replaced positional or ambiguous
  accommodation headers with listening/speaking- and reading-specific names;
- positions 301-306, 501-504, and 701-706: removed generated
  `reporting_category_1` spillover and preserved the source's plural reporting
  category scores; and
- history positions 1003-1011, 1042-1050, and 1075-1083: removed duplicated
  `cdc` wording while retaining year-specific county-district-campus meaning.

No source-supported change was required to a mapped range, length, ordering,
or ordinal. No named source row was omitted, and no blank source row was
mapped.

### Metadata and filename evidence

The source URL was updated to the verified redirect destination. Page 1 states
that delivery is a fixed-length text file and that the extension will be
`.txt`. The four inherited filename regexes previously allowed a missing
extension; each now requires `.txt`.

The layout does not document delivered basenames. The inherited basename
patterns and their existing external references were therefore retained as
provenance-limited patterns; they were not treated as same-year source facts.
No same-year evidence directly contradicted the retained basename forms.

## Limitations and Historical-Data Decision

- No operational 2021 delivery sample was available. Delivered basenames,
  record contents, and parser behavior against an actual delivery remain
  unverified.
- The PDF supports the `.txt` suffix but not the inherited basename patterns.
- No historical data reprocessing decision is required by this repository-only
  mapping correction. Downstream users should assess reprocessing needs if they
  previously depended on the corrected header names or extension-optional
  regex behavior.
