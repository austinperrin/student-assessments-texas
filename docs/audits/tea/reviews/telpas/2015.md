## Findings and Corrections

### Source terminology and shared normalizations

The following source-supported header corrections were applied:

- position 117: `gifted_talented_indicator_code` to
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`;
- position 122: removed abbreviation and the printed code while preserving the
  source's grades 6-12 applicability as
  `career_and_technical_education_indicator_code_grades_6_12`;
- positions 135-137: replaced erroneous/generated Agency Use headers with
  `agency_use_column_c` through `agency_use_column_e`;
- positions 155-163: replaced the positional placeholder with
  `fall_2014_peims_county_district_campus_number`;
- positions 174-175: removed unsupported `tsds` and `_1` additions from the
  single Fall 2014 PEIMS crisis-code field;
- positions 181 and 191: removed duplicated `rater` spillover from the Rater
  Info A and B headers;
- positions 232, 247, 262, and 277: replaced positional duplicate suffixes
  with the source-supported domain score-code-default meanings;
- positions 291-323: replaced positional disambiguators with the source's
  Beginning, Intermediate, Advanced, and Advanced High proficiency-level
  contexts;
- positions 553-622: clarified that the reading student-item-response value
  indicates whether each response was correct or incorrect by using
  `reading_student_item_response_correctness_indicator`; and
- positions 675-753: made the source's TELPAS meaning explicit for each
  year-qualified composite-rating and reading-history field.

No source-supported change was required to a mapped range, length, order, or
ordinal. No named source row was omitted, and no blank source row was mapped.

### Metadata and filename evidence

The metadata identifies Spring 2015 (`0315`), school year 2014-2015, and the
verified current same-year URL. Reviewer 2 found that the prior
`student-assessment` route returned HTTP 404 and verified that the replacement
`data-reports` URL serves a PDF that byte-matches the archive. The source
discusses fixed-width positions and an optional tab-delimited delivery but does
not document delivered basenames or file extensions.

The four inherited filename patterns and external references were retained
unchanged as provenance-limited patterns. Same-year evidence neither supports
their basenames nor directly contradicts them, so the audit did not infer a
replacement convention.

## Reviewer 2 Disposition and Reviewer 1 Response

Reviewer 2 first held sign-off pending one 2015 correction. The old metadata URL
under `student-assessment/student-assessment-results` returned HTTP 404, while
the current URL under `data-reports/student-assessment-results` resolved and
byte-matched the archived PDF.

Reviewer 1 replaced the stale URL in the mapping and this review record and
updated the source evidence above with Reviewer 2's independently verified
byte count and SHA-256. No mapping row, range, ordinal, or header changed.

Reviewer 2's subsequent semantic review requested two mapping-header
corrections. At position 122, the prior header omitted the source's explicit
grades 6-12 applicability. At positions 553-622, the prior
`reading_student_item_response` header could imply that the range stores the
answer itself, while the source overview explains that the range indicates
whether each response was correct or incorrect. Reviewer 1 changed the two
headers to
`career_and_technical_education_indicator_code_grades_6_12` and
`reading_student_item_response_correctness_indicator`, respectively. No range,
length, order, or source ordinal changed. Reviewer 2 also stated that complete
independent source-row and semantic reconciliation remained pending at that
stage.

Reviewer 2 then completed the independent review of live head `d1f57ba` and
approved the work unit. The review reconciled all 146 source rows as 117 mapped
named rows plus 29 omitted blanks through position 800 and ordinal 146;
confirmed both requested semantic corrections; independently matched the
official PDF and archive at 1,069,264 bytes and SHA-256
`401B755681527B1CD65C295B6CA4B9250AB321550EC262D1868AA336C489E52B`;
and confirmed the mapping invariants, current-head GitHub validation, and the
documented filename and operational-sample limitations. No further correction
was requested.
