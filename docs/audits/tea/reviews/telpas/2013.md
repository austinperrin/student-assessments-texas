## Findings and corrections

### Omitted named fields and incorrect ordinals — high severity

- Source: pages 4–5 and 8–9, positions 96, 108, 231, 246, 261, and 276; page 13, positions 664–753.
- Finding: the mapping omitted Black/African American and bilingual indicators, all four domain score-code fields, and all 30 Spring 2008–2012 TELPAS history fields. Later `column_num` values therefore did not represent source-row ordinals.
- Correction: added all 36 named fields and recalculated every `column_num` against all 142 source rows. The final mapped field, `period` at position 800, is ordinal 142.
- Resolution: corrected and verified against the cited source rows.

### Score code/default meaning — high severity

- Source: pages 8–9, positions 231–233, 246–248, 261–263, and 276–278.
- Finding: positions 232, 247, 262, and 277 were labeled as score codes even though the source identifies them as score-code defaults; the actual score codes at positions 231, 246, 261, and 276 were absent.
- Correction: added the four score-code fields and renamed the four default fields with `_score_code_default`. Position 278 now retains the source's TELPAS-specific reading proficiency label.
- Resolution: corrected and verified.

### Ambiguous or lossy headers — medium severity

- Source: pages 5–6 and 10, positions 117, 127–137, 141, 155–163, and 291–325.
- Finding: several headers omitted distinguishing source meaning or used position suffixes to avoid collisions.
- Correction: preserved “gifted and talented,” “header agency use,” the grades 1–12 scope for years in U.S. schools, the Fall 2012 PEIMS CDC meaning, and the proficiency-level context for repeated reporting-category fields.
- Resolution: corrected and verified; all headers are unique lowercase snake case and no longer than 63 characters.

### Official URL relocation — low severity

- Finding: the recorded TEA URL no longer served the PDF. TEA's current official result points to the `data-reports` URL with the `-2.pdf` basename.
- Correction: updated `metadata.pdf_url`. The downloaded official file byte-matches the existing archive.
- Resolution: corrected and verified.

## Filename and operational evidence

The 2013 layout does not document a delivered filename or basename. The inherited `filename_patterns` are retained to avoid an unsupported compatibility change, but their references are later/general external guidance rather than same-year filename evidence. They establish syntax only imperfectly and do not prove actual 2013 delivered basenames. All regexes compile.

No de-identified or production 2013 operational sample was available. Consequently, field values, padding behavior, delivered filename matching, and downstream parsing were not sample-validated. This is a disclosed evidence limitation, not a substitute for the completed source-layout review.

## Historical-data decision

The corrected mapping is source-accurate but materially expands the represented schema and changes source ordinals. Consumers that previously ingested 2013 TELPAS files should reprocess or otherwise reconcile historical extracts if they require the restored demographics, score codes, or TELPAS history fields. No operational data was changed by this pull request.
