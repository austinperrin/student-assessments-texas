## Complete Source Reconciliation

Reviewer 1 inspected the PDF table, notes, and codes directly, using temporary
coordinate-aware extraction only to support exhaustive accounting. The source
contains 158 positional rows spanning positions 1 through 800:

- 125 named rows are present in `mapped_fields`.
- 33 blank rows are omitted from `mapped_fields` and remain represented by gaps
  in `column_num`.
- All source ranges agree with their printed lengths, are ordered, and cover
  positions 1-800 without overlap or an unexplained gap.
- Every named row is mapped, and no blank row is mapped.
- The final source row is `PERIOD`, position 800, source ordinal 158.

The audit included administration and identifiers, demographics, other student
information, rater information, reading accommodations, ELL progress-measure

## Findings And Corrections

### Source ordinals in TELPAS history

- Status: corrected
- Severity: high
- Evidence: PDF pages 12-13, positions 647-800.
- Finding: the 30 mapped Spring 2012-2016 history fields had incorrect and in
  several cases duplicated `column_num` values. The terminal period field used
  ordinal 127 instead of 158.
- Resolution: positions 664-753 now use consecutive source ordinals 127-156;
  blank positions 754-799 retain ordinal 157; position 800 uses ordinal 158.

### Source-supported field meanings

- Status: corrected
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: PDF pages 4-12, positions 117, 133-137, 155-163, 201-203,
  227-230, 291-323, and 630.
- Finding: 26 headers either used generated positional suffixes, omitted a
  source-defined context, or did not use a required shared normalization.
- Resolution:
  - normalized position 117 to `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`;
  - restored Agency Use A-E at positions 133-137;
  - qualified position 155 as the Fall 2016 PEIMS campus number;
  - preserved the reading context for accommodations at positions 201-203;
  - preserved plan-year meaning at positions 227-230 and distinguished it from
    the current TELPAS composite rating at position 630; and
  - replaced positional reporting-category disambiguators at positions
    291-323 with beginning, intermediate, advanced, and advanced-high
    proficiency-level terminology supported by the source.

These corrections change output keys but do not change fixed-width extraction
ranges. Consumers of prior keys may need aliases or reprocessing under their
local retention policy.

### Official source metadata

- Status: corrected
- Severity: low
- Evidence: TEA's current Data File Formats Archive identifies the 2017 TELPAS
  PDF at the current `/data-reports/student-assessment-results/` route.
- Finding: `metadata.pdf_url` used TEA's superseded route.
- Resolution: the metadata now uses the current official URL, whose bytes match
  the archived source.

### Filename extension

- Status: corrected with provenance limitation
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: PDF page 1 states twice that the file extension is `.dat`.
- Finding: all four inherited regexes allowed an optional `.txt` extension,
  directly contradicting the same-year source.
- Resolution: every regex now requires `.dat`. The inherited basename forms
  were retained because the layout does not document delivered basenames and
  same-year evidence does not directly contradict those forms.

## Source Notes And Codes

- Administration code `0317` means Spring 2017.
- Reading accommodations are distinct from TELPAS domain ratings.
- Listening, speaking, writing, and reading score codes and proficiency ratings
  remain separate concepts.
- The TELPAS comprehension score, composite score, and composite rating remain
  separate fields with their source-defined meanings.
- Student item responses at positions 553-622 contain response characters in
  2017; the overview expressly distinguishes them from correctness flags.
- Spring 2012-2016 grade, campus, composite-rating, reading-scale-score,
  reading-proficiency-rating, and reading-score-code fields remain year
  qualified.

## Limitations

- No operational 2017 data file was available. Real-world record contents and
  parser behavior could not be tested.
- The source establishes the `.dat` extension but does not establish delivered
  basenames. The retained basename patterns therefore remain inherited,
  provenance-limited guidance rather than verified 2017 delivery evidence.
- The non-TEA references attached to inherited basename regexes were not used
  as evidence for source rows or field meanings.
