## Complete Source Reconciliation

The source contains 158 field rows: 116 named rows mapped and 42 blank rows
omitted. Together they cover positions 1-1200 without gaps or overlaps. Every
range length agrees with its printed endpoints and length. `column_num`
preserves all source-row ordinals, including gaps for the 42 omitted blanks;
position 1200 (`period`) is source ordinal 158.

All source sections were reviewed:

- administration, student identification, demographics, and other student
  information, positions 1-200;
- rater and accommodations information, positions 201-260;
- score codes and proficiency ratings, positions 261-300;
- listening, speaking, and reading subject information, positions 301-901;
- comprehension and composite data, positions 902-1000;
- Spring 2013 through Spring 2017 history, positions 1001-1175; and
- the final blank and period rows, positions 1176-1200.

The four domain score-code and proficiency-rating concepts remain distinct.
Listening, speaking, and reading reporting-category, raw-score, scale-score,
administration, and item-level meanings are preserved. Comprehension score,
composite score, and composite rating remain separate. Each historical block
retains its administration year and distinct reading and composite fields.

## Findings and Corrections

### Source terminology and shared normalizations

The following source-supported header corrections were applied:

- position 107: `lep_indicator_code` to
  `emergent_bilingual_indicator_code`, applying the required shared
  normalization to the source's LEP indicator concept;
- position 117: `gifted_talented_indicator_code` to
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`;
- positions 133-137: replaced five erroneous/generated Agency Use headers
  with `agency_use_a` through `agency_use_e`, matching source columns A-E;
- positions 155-163: replaced the positional placeholder with
  `fall_2017_peims_county_district_campus_number`;
- positions 174-175: removed unsupported `tsds` and `_1` additions from the
  single source field, producing `fall_2017_peims_crisis_code`;
- positions 221, 222, 241, 242, and 243: replaced ambiguous or positional
  accommodation headers with listening/speaking- and reading-specific names;
  and
- positions 301-306, 501-504, and 701-706: removed generated
  `reporting_category_1` spillover and preserved the source's plural reporting
  category scores.

No source-supported change was required to a mapped range, length, order, or
ordinal. No named source row was omitted, and no blank source row was mapped.

### Metadata and filename evidence

The metadata URL was updated from the nonworking historical path to the current
official URL linked by TEA's archive page. Page 1 states that delivery is a
fixed-length text file and that the extension will be `.dat`; tab-delimited
files are unavailable. All four inherited filename regexes previously allowed
optional `.txt`; each now requires `.dat`.

The layout does not document delivered basenames. The inherited basename
patterns and external references were retained as provenance-limited patterns,
consistent with the workflow requirement not to change a pattern without
same-year contrary evidence. Only the directly contradicted suffix was changed.
