## Findings And Corrections

### Source ordinals omitted blank rows

- Finding: The original mapping numbered mapped entries consecutively instead
  of preserving source-row ordinals after blank rows. The first divergence was
  the page 3 blank at positions 71-90 (source row 10); 669 mapped entries had an
  incorrect `column_num`.
- Correction: Reconciled every entry to the complete 874-row source sequence.
  All 93 blank rows now produce the required ordinal gaps.
- Resolution: corrected and ready for independent verification.

### Named source fields were omitted

- Finding: 103 named source rows on pages 8-37 were absent. These included
  Approaches Grade Level, High School Equivalency Program, accommodations,
  performance-level and raw-score fields, test-form and prior-year indicators,
  cumulative-history alternate/substitute assessment fields, and two-year
  prior score-code fields.
- Correction: Added every omitted named row with its documented position,
  meaning, section context, and source ordinal. Representative affected
  positions include 279, 288, 314, 319, 646, 708-709, 745, 850-851, 932-933,
  1237, 1249-1250, 1299-1300, 1319-1320, 1369-1370, 1389-1390, 1439-1440,
  1509-1510, 1529-1530, 1579-1580, 1919, 1930-1937, 1939-1942, 1963,
  1966-1969, and 1991-1997.
- Resolution: corrected and ready for independent verification.

### Positions 1929-1939 were misrepresented

- Finding: On pages 35-36, the mapping combined positions 1929-1932 as a
  four-character mathematics administration date. The source instead defines
  separate one-character score-code rows at positions 1929-1937, a blank at
  1938, and the reading administration date at positions 1939-1942.
- Correction: Replaced the combined field with the nine documented score-code
  rows, omitted the documented blank, and mapped the administration date at
  its actual range.
- Resolution: corrected and ready for independent verification.

### Generated header debris obscured source meaning

- Finding: Ten headers ended in generated hexadecimal fragments rather than a
  stable representation of the documented title. Affected positions were 205,
  245-249, 424-428, 603-607, 937-940, 1087-1090, and 2005.
- Correction: Replaced the fragments with concise source-supported names for
  the PEIMS snapshot status, administration dates, record-update indicators,
  and SIFE field. Meaningful family/administration distinctions were retained.
- Resolution: corrected and ready for independent verification.
