## Complete Coverage Reconciliation

The source contains 845 fixed-width source rows spanning positions 1–2206:

- 744 named rows, all mapped;
- 101 `Blank` rows, all omitted from `mapped_fields` while retained as positional and `column_num` gaps;
- no mapped blank row;
- no omitted named row after correction; and
- every source position is covered exactly once by either a mapped named field or an omitted blank field.

The source's page 38 row for Writing Tested Grade prints an end position of
`2551`, but gives length 2, starts at 2150, places the next row at 2152, and
continues the record through position 2206. The mapping therefore retains
2150–2151. This is a source-supported reconciliation of an internally
inconsistent printed end position, not evidence from another year.

The source's page 13 blank row at positions 600–602 prints length `5`, although
its endpoints span three positions and the following row starts at 603. The
blank remains omitted, and positions 600–602 are used for coverage because the
printed endpoints and next-row boundary agree. This source inconsistency does
not require a mapping-range or source-ordinal change.

### Source ordinal preservation

- Severity: high
- State: corrected; pending independent verification
- Evidence: all 845 source rows on pages 3–40

The previous mapping numbered mapped fields consecutively and did not preserve
source-row gaps for omitted blanks. The first mismatch occurred at position 91;
648 of the 657 pre-audit mapped entries had an incorrect `column_num` under the
repository rule. All 744 mapped fields now use their actual source ordinal,
including gaps for all 101 blank rows. The final mapped field at position 2206
is source row 845.

### Generated header artifacts

- Severity: medium
- State: corrected; pending independent verification
- Evidence: source field titles at positions 205, 245, 249, 424, 428, 603,
  607, 937, 1087, and 2005

Ten headers contained generated six-character hash suffixes rather than
source-derived terminology. They were replaced with readable snake-case names
that preserve the source section and field meaning. All headers remain unique
and no longer than 63 characters.
