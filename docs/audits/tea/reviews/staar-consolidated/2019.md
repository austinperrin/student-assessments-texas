## Findings And Resolutions

### TEA-STAAR-CAF-2019-001: Replace obsolete official URL

- Severity: medium
- Source fact: the initial metadata URL under TEA's former
  `/student-assessment/` path returns 404. The same-year PDF is available from
  TEA's current `/data-reports/` path.
- Correction: updated `metadata.pdf_url` to the current official TEA URL.
- Verification: the downloaded current PDF and archive have identical SHA-256
  hashes.
- Final status: corrected and verified by Reviewer 1

### TEA-STAAR-CAF-2019-002: Replace opaque generated header suffixes

- Severity: medium
- Source fact: the source supplies meaningful field names and section context
  for every named row.
- Initial implementation: 54 headers ended in opaque six-character hash
  suffixes introduced by truncation rather than source terminology.
- Correction: replaced those headers with concise source-supported names that
  retain the applicable program, subject, administration, and cumulative-
  history distinctions while remaining unique and at most 63 characters.
- Final status: corrected and verified by Reviewer 1

### TEA-STAAR-CAF-2019-003: Resolve printed blank-range endpoint typo

- Severity: medium
- Source fact: page 20 prints the blank after position 818 as positions
  `819-922` with length 4, while the next named row begins at position 823.
- Resolution: the internally consistent blank range is 819-822. The blank was
  already omitted from `mapped_fields`, ordinal 301 already begins at position
  823, and no mapping-range or ordinal correction was required.
- Final status: confirmed and documented by Reviewer 1
