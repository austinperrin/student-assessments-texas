- Severity: low

- Correction: Changed it to `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`; positions and `column_num` remain unchanged.

### ALT2-EOC-2022-002 — Family Portal access-code normalization

- Severity: low
- Source fact: PDF page 5, positions 157-162, length 6 is “Student Portal Unique Access Code”; its note says the code provides families confidential access through the Family Portal.
- Finding: The header `student_portal_unique_access_code` used a legacy variant despite the source independently supporting the shared Family Portal concept.
- Correction: Changed it to `family_portal_unique_access_code`; positions and `column_num` remain unchanged.
- Final status: corrected and verified
