## Findings And Resolutions

### ALT2-EOC-2018-01 — Incorrect source-row ordinals

- Severity: high
- Evidence: The 2018 layout includes blank rows at positions 112–116, 119–121, 132–143, 164, 166–189, 207–214, 220–290, 292–305, 307, 309–350, 392–399, 410, 412, 417–700, and 703. The existing `column_num` sequence did not consistently count those rows and became duplicate or decreasing at positions 117, 191, 391–400, and 414–701.
- Resolution: Corrected every affected ordinal. The final mapping uses source ordinals 1–94, with 21 gaps corresponding exactly to the 21 omitted `BLANK` rows.
- Status: corrected; pending Reviewer 2 verification.

### ALT2-EOC-2018-02 — Required shared header normalizations

- Severity: medium
- Evidence: PDF pages 4–5 identify `LEP-INDICATOR-CODE`, `GIFTED-TALENTED-INDICATOR-CODE`, and `STUDENT PORTAL UNIQUE ACCESS CODE`; their meanings support the repository-required recurring-concept normalizations.
- Resolution: Changed the headers to `emergent_bilingual_indicator_code`, `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`, and `family_portal_unique_access_code`.
- Status: corrected; pending Reviewer 2 verification.

### ALT2-EOC-2018-03 — Filename extension contradicted the source

- Severity: medium
- Evidence: PDF page 2 states twice that the fixed-length file extension is `.dat`. Three inherited regexes explicitly allowed `.txt` instead.
- Resolution: Changed those explicit suffix alternatives from `.txt` to `.dat`. Retained the inherited basename patterns because the layout provides no basename convention.
- Status: corrected; pending Reviewer 2 verification.
