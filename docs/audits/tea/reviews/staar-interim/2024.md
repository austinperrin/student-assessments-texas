## Findings And Resolutions

### 1. Segment qualifiers were missing from nine headers

- State: `verified`
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: pages 1-4, positions 510-629 and 1250-2149.
- Source fact: segment 2 is labeled `HIGH`, segment 3 `MEDIUM`, and segment 4 `LOW` in both the item response/score rows and the SE-alignment rows.
- Resolution: restored the source-supported qualifiers to six item response/score headers and three SE-alignment headers.

### 2. Performance and category indicator meanings were truncated

- State: `verified`
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: pages 2-3, positions 630-724.
- Source fact: position 630-670 is `Predicted STAAR Performance Level`; positions 683-718 are reporting-category strength/weakness indicators; positions 719-724 are historical reporting-category strength/weakness indicators.
- Resolution: added `level` to the performance header and restored `strength_weakness_indicator` to all twelve category headers.

### 3. Two recurring identifiers did not use required normalized names

- State: `verified`
- Severity: low
- Evidence: page 5, positions 2181 and 2185, together with the repository normalization rules.
- Source fact: the LEP row describes the same emergent-bilingual status concept, and the gifted-talented row is the recurring gifted-and-talented indicator concept.
- Resolution: renamed the headers to `emergent_bilingual_indicator_code` and `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code` without changing their positions or source meanings.

### 4. Two accessibility headers omitted wrapped source text

- State: `verified`
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: page 6, positions 2204-2205.
- Source fact: the complete field titles are `Content-Language Accommodations` and `Braille/Refreshable Braille Device Screen Reader Support`.
- Resolution: restored the complete concepts as `content_language_accommodations` and `braille_refreshable_braille_device_screen_reader_support`.

In total, 26 source-supported header corrections were applied. No positional, width, ordering, ordinal, metadata, or test-code changes were required.

## Filename Provenance And Limitations

The source identifies a fixed-width student-results layout but supplies no delivered basename, filename convention, or regex. The mapping therefore does not add `filename_patterns`. No operational 2023-2024 STAAR Interim result file or delivered filename sample was available, so filename matching and record-value validation could not be independently tested. This is a documented evidence limitation, not an accepted block on the source-layout audit.

The official URL was unavailable at review time. The archived PDF is internally identified as the assigned school-year layout, but a current byte-for-byte official/archive comparison could not be performed.
