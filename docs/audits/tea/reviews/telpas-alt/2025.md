## Findings and resolutions

### Reporting-category field titles

- State: `corrected`
- Severity: low
- Source: pages 7-8, positions 301-304, 501-504, 701-706, and 753-756.
- Finding: nine mapping headers omitted the final word `Score` from named
  source titles such as `Listening Reporting Category 1 Score`.
- Resolution: added `_score` to all nine affected listening, speaking,
  reading, and writing reporting-category headers. Positions, lengths, and
  ordinals were unchanged.

### Gifted/talented shared normalization

- State: `corrected`
- Severity: low
- Source: page 4, position 117.
- Finding: the source's `Gifted-Talented-Indicator-Code` expresses the shared
  gifted-and-talented concept, but the mapping used
  `gifted_talented_indicator_code`.
- Resolution: renamed it to the required shared normalization
  `gifted_and_talented_indicator_code`. Position, length, and ordinal were
  unchanged.

No other source-supported mapping correction was found.

## Metadata and filename evidence

Metadata values agree with the assigned file, the 2024-2025 layout title, and
the Spring 2025 administration code `0325`. The archived layout does not state
a delivered basename convention. Existing `filename_patterns` were therefore
retained as inherited compatibility metadata, not confirmed from the assigned
layout. Their cited external operational pages and later naming-convention
reference were not used as 2025 audit evidence. The regexes compile, but actual
delivery-name matching could not be tested because no operational sample was
available.

## Historical-data and reprocessing decision

The corrections change headers only; byte ranges and source ordinals are
unchanged. Consumers selecting the ten old header names must adopt the
corrected names. Stored raw fixed-width files do not require reprocessing
unless downstream schemas materialize those header names.
