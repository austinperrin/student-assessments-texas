external naming guidance; the cited 2025–2026 convention is later than this
administration, and the other references do not provide a same-year delivered
basename in the reviewed evidence. All five regexes compile, but actual 2023
delivery-name coverage remains provenance-limited pending an operational sample
or same-year naming document.
